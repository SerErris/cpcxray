# Third-party material shipped with cpcxray

## Amstrad CPC ROMs (`src/cpcxray/roms/`)

`os464.rom`, `os664.rom`, `os6128.rom` (operating system), `basic10.rom`, `basic11_664.rom`,
`basic11_6128.rom` (Locomotive BASIC 1.0 / 1.1) and `amsdos.rom` (AMSDOS).

Copyright © Amstrad Consumer Electronics plc and Locomotive Software Ltd. The images are
unmodified; their copyright messages are kept. They are shipped because Amstrad allowed the CPC
ROMs to be distributed with emulators (the project owner regards them as freely
distributable). They are not covered by cpcxray's GPL licence. No other ROM and no disk image
is shipped.
